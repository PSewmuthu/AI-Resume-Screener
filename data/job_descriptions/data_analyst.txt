Job Title: Data Analyst — Recruitment Analytics Team
Company: Horizon Talent Solutions (recruitment & HR technology)

About the role:
We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our Recruitment Analytics team.
You will work with candidate, client, and job-posting data to build reports
and dashboards that help our recruiters make faster, better-informed decisions.

Responsibilities:
- Clean, transform, and analyze structured recruitment datasets (candidate
  profiles, job postings, placement outcomes) using Python and SQL.
- Build and maintain dashboards (Power BI or similar) tracking key
  recruitment metrics such as time-to-fill, source effectiveness, and
  candidate pipeline health.
- Work with the data science team to support forecasting and reporting
  projects.
- Communicate findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders such as
  recruiters and HR managers.
- Document data pipelines and maintain basic data quality checks.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Data Science, or a
  related field (or currently pursuing one).
- Solid Python skills (pandas, basic OOP) and working SQL knowledge.
- Experience building dashboards or reports (Power BI, Tableau, or similar).
- Familiarity with basic statistics and data visualization best practices.
- Strong communication skills; comfortable explaining data insights to
  non-technical audiences.

Nice to have:
- Exposure to NLP or resume-parsing tools.
- Experience with time-series forecasting (e.g. Prophet, SARIMAX).
- Prior internship or project experience in the recruitment/HR domain.
